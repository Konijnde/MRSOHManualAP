# Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope (Manual)
Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope is a 2022 action-adventure tactical role-playing game developed by Ubisoft Milan and Ubisoft Paris and published by Ubisoft for the Nintendo Switch. The game is a crossover between Nintendo's Mario and Ubisoft's Rabbids franchises and a sequel to Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle.

<sup> Source: Wikipedia

## Rules
Your goal is to defeat the final boss of the game, Cursa. To do so, you will have to collect Characters, Sparks and Purified Darkmess Energy Crystals to progress through the game's 5 planets.

You will start the game with 2 randomly selected characters from the initial 6 available. You may only use characters whose item you have received in battles, with the following exceptions:
- You may play the tutorial battles regardless of which characters you possess
- You may play the boss battles where you unlock Edge, Rabbid Rosalina and Bowser even if you do not have their item

To fight Cursa, you must have all 9 characters.

Similarly, you may only equip Sparks whose item you have received. You are allowed to use Exosphere in Driven to the Edge, as it is locked into your team for that battle.

The Beep-O abilities, Wave, Scan and Wave Upgrade, may only be used when you have the respective item.

The major sub areas on each planet require their respective key to enter:
- Sunrise Temple and the Lighthouse on Beacon Beach
- The Winter Palace on Pristine Peaks
- The Secret Passage and Spellbound Woods on Palette Prime
- The Everbloom Tree and Mt. Spout on Terra Flora
- The Industrial Area and Garage on Barrendale Mesa

To go to the next planet, you will need 2/4/6/8/10 Purified Darkmess Energy Crystals and 3/6/9/12/15 usable Sparks for Pristine Peaks/Palette Prime/Terra Flora/Barrendale Mesa/Cursa's Stronghold. A Spark is usable if you both have it unlocked in-game and have its respective Archipelago item.

You are allowed to use any items you find on the map or obtain randomly by completing battles. For each Archipelago item corresponding to an in-game item, you may buy one copy of it from Salesbot 9.99+TX.

## Locations
- Main story battles
- Planet coin and spark side quest completions
- Checkpoints
- Memory probes
- Purchasable memories from Salesbot 9.99+TX
Weapon unlocks, whether purchasable, found on the map, rewarded for the Secret Zone time trials or for 100% completing a planet, do not count as locations. Collectible items also do not count as locations.

## Battle logic
This game is pretty easy, so the logic doesn't expect you to have much to beat a specific battle. It only expects you to have at least one defensive character (Peach, Rabbid Peach, Rabbid Luigi or Rabbid Rosalina) in order to beat most of the Survive battles.

## Notes
This game is quite long, so it's probably not viable for sync games. It is recommended to play on Relaxing or Average difficulty, because playing on Demanding with a weak team can lead to extremely grindy battles.
